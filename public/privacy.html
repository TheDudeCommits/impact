IMPACT / LEGAL & PRIVACY

Legal & Privacy

Impact is a free, unofficial, fan-made browser game. This page says, in plain words, what it keeps about you, why, who helps run it, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated 24 October 2026.

What this game is

  • Impact is purely a fan game, made by lovers of anime and manga who grew up watching these characters day in and day out. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Shueisha, Kodansha, Hakusensha, Toei Animation, Bandai Namco or any other rights holder of the series it celebrates. All characters, names and marks belong to their respective owners.
  • It is open source (the repository on GitHub) and it has no monetization whatsoever: nothing is sold, there are no ads, and there never will be, in any form or shape.
  • Rights holders and creators: if you are an IP owner, or someone whose models, effects or other assets have been used in the game, and you would like them removed, email Hello@Dude.work and they will be removed promptly.
  • Where the game's material comes from is listed on the credits page. What you can expect of the game, and what it expects of you, is in the terms of use.

The short version

  • You can play without signing up. Your account is a random ID and a token kept in your browser.
  • If you sign in, our server keeps your Privy user ID, never your email address, phone or social handle.
  • Nothing is sold. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers. When something in the game breaks, it sends our own server an error report that says what broke, never who you are. You can turn that off in OPTIONS → SEND ERROR REPORTS.
  • You can delete your account at any time: ACCOUNT → PROFILE → DELETE ACCOUNT, or by email.

01Who runs it

Impact, at impact.dude.work. For anything about your data, write to Hello@Dude.work.

02What is kept

In your browser, on your device only:

  • your account's token (a random code that opens your account) and a copy of the account, so the game can show it at once;
  • your settings and controls, your career records, and the name you type to play under;
  • if you signed in: how you signed in, shown masked (for example “with Google” or “a•••@g•••.com”), so the ACCOUNT tab can say so. It is never sent to our server.

On our game server:

  • Every player: a random account ID, your token stored only as a one-way hash, and when the account was made.
  • If you sign in: your Privy user ID (a random ID that Privy gives your login), so the same account opens on every device you sign in on. Privy itself holds your email address or social login (Google, X or Discord) in order to sign you in. When you redeem a code that belongs to one email address, the server reads that address from Privy's signed confirmation to check it, and does not keep it.
  • A username, if you claim one. Other players see it in matches.
  • Gameplay records: the online matches you finish and the Sumi they earned, your Sumi, level, missions and feats, the items you have unlocked and wear, and the codes you have redeemed. Each change is written to a log, so a crash loses nothing.

While you play online, the match server receives the name you play under, your fighter, what you wear and your inputs, and the other players in that match see your name, fighter and look. A match is held in memory and gone when it ends; only its result is added to your account.

Your IP address is used in the server's memory to limit abuse (how many accounts, codes and deletions one address may try in an hour, and how many error reports it may send). It is not written to your account. Our hosting providers keep short-lived server logs, which can include IP addresses, for security and to fix faults.

Bug reports you make stay on your device: you download or copy them yourself. The game does not upload them.

Error reports are sent by the game itself when something breaks (an error in its code, the graphics failing, a match that would not load), so that it can be fixed. One goes only to our own game server, no other company. It holds what went wrong and where in the game's code, the version of the game, the page and the screen you were on (for example the home or a match), your browser's name and version and your device's system (for example “Chrome 141 / Android”, never the full text your browser sends), and a random number made for that browser tab. It never holds your IP address, your account or its token, your username or the name you play under, or your email address: before it is sent, and again on the server, anything that looks like a token, an email address or an ID is taken out. Error reports are kept for 30 days, then deleted, and are used only to find and fix what broke. Nothing is sent before the first-visit notice has been shown to you. To stop them, turn off SEND ERROR REPORTS in OPTIONS (it is in the pause menu too); then nothing is sent.

03What is not done

  • Nothing about you is sold or shared for advertising.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Error reports (see What is kept) say what broke, never who it broke for, and are used for nothing else.
  • Nothing is sold for money. Sumi is earned by playing and cannot be bought.

04Who helps run it

  • Privy runs sign-in (email, Google, X or Discord). Its code loads only when you use sign-in: to sign in or out, or to delete a signed-in account. Privy's own privacy policy covers what it holds.
  • Railway hosts the game server, in the Netherlands (Amsterdam), which keeps the accounts.
  • Vercel hosts the website.

05How long it is kept, and deleting it

Your account is kept while it exists. To delete it, open ACCOUNT → PROFILE → DELETE ACCOUNT and confirm. That erases, from the server: your account and its records, your Sumi and items, your username (anyone may claim it afterwards), the link to your Privy login, and every token that opened it, on every device. Its entries are taken out of the server's log as well. The browser then signs out of Privy and starts again as a new guest. It cannot be undone.

Guests can delete their accounts the same way. If you can no longer reach your account (for example, you cleared your browser's data), email Hello@Dude.work with the account ID shown under ACCOUNT, or with how you signed in, and it will be deleted for you.

Backups of the server's storage, made before updates so that a fault can be undone, may hold a deleted account until they expire. Server logs are kept briefly. Deleting your game account does not delete your Privy login: ask Privy for that.

06Age

Impact is meant for players aged 13 and over. If you are younger, or below the age your country sets for this, play only with a parent's or guardian's permission.

07Changes

If what the game keeps changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top moves. This version is dated 18 October 2026.
